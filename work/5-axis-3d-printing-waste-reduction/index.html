ACADEMIC RESEARCH · ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING

Reducing Plastic Waste with 5D Printing

Prototype research exploring support-material alternatives and multi-axis printing as approaches to reducing waste.

Context

Academic research at Johnson & Wales University. Co-authored and presented.

My contribution

Research, prototype development, fabrication exploration and presentation as part of the project team.

Stage

Feasibility research. This portfolio does not present the work as a validated commercial manufacturing process.

THE QUESTION

Can changing how a part is oriented during printing reduce dependence on disposable supports?

Conventional additive workflows can require temporary support structures for difficult geometry. The project investigated support-material alternatives and multi-axis approaches as possible ways to address that waste. Earlier portfolio versions included conflicting percentage-reduction claims; those figures are intentionally omitted here until the underlying measurements and comparison conditions are confirmed.
