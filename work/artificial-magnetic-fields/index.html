ARCHIVE · ACADEMIC CONCEPT STUDY

Artificial Magnetic Fields for Mars

A speculative Rhino and Grasshopper study exploring how a deployable device and field visualization might be represented parametrically.

Stage

Academic computational design concept and simulation study.

Tools

Rhino 3D and Grasshopper, based on the existing project materials.

Limit

No radiation-shielding performance, engineering feasibility, or physical validation is claimed.

Why it remains in the archive

The project shows parametric design exploration and visual communication. Older portfolio copy described physically comparable magnetic fields and deployable engineering performance; those claims are removed because this portfolio does not contain evidence sufficient to support them.
