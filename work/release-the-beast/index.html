The Idea

"Release the Beast" isn't about aggression — it's about discipline, momentum, and pushing past self-imposed limits. The brand targets people who train hard, stay consistent, and want apparel that matches their intensity without being over-the-top.

The design goal: aggressive but clean. Every graphic had to read clearly on apparel at a distance, scale across product lines (shirts, shorts, sports bras), and hold up in both monochrome and accent-color treatments.

Brand System

  • Mark: Aggressive icon silhouette — works as a chest print, tag label, or standalone emblem.
  • Wordmark: High-contrast lettering, legible at small sizes on labels and large on banners.
  • Style: Monochrome base + red accent for "energy" — keeps production simple while maintaining visual punch.
