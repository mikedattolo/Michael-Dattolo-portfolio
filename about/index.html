About

Who I Am

I'm Michael Dattolo—a product designer and innovator based in Hackettstown, NJ. I hold a B.S. in Integrated Product Design from Johnson & Wales University (GPA 3.9, Dean's List, 2024).

Currently, I work as a Special Operations Specialist at Techflex, where I manage precision equipment operations and contribute to manufacturing optimization and continuous improvement.

I focus on designing new solutions and optimizing existing products—turning complex constraints into clear concepts, functional prototypes, and market-ready iterations. My work spans manufacturing, robotics, medical devices, and emerging technologies.

Background

How I Work
