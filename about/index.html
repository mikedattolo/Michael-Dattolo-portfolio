About

Short Story

I'm a product designer and prototyper based in Hackettstown, NJ. I earned my B.S. in Integrated Product Design from Johnson & Wales University (GPA 3.9, Dean's List). I'm currently a Special Operations Specialist at Techflex, where I work hands-on with manufacturing processes and continuous improvement.

My work spans autonomous robotics, sustainable fabrication, and sensor-driven systems—always grounded in the question: does it actually work? I build end-to-end: concept → CAD → prototype → testing → iteration, documenting decisions and tradeoffs along the way.
