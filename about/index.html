About

Short Story

I’m a product designer and prototyper studying at Johnson & Wales University, where I’ve spent the last two years turning ideas into real, testable systems. My work spans autonomous robotics, sustainable fabrication, and sensor-driven agriculture—always grounded in the question: does it actually work?

I build things end-to-end: concept sketches, 3D simulation in Rhino and Grasshopper, microcontroller firmware on Raspberry Pi, ROS 2 robot packages in Gazebo, and front-end dashboards in HTML/CSS/JS. I enjoy the moments where design meets engineering constraints, because that’s where the most honest decisions happen.

Skills

CAD / Fabrication

UX / Product

Tools

  • ROS 2, Gazebo, CMake, AI-assisted workflows (ComfyUI)
  • FastAPI, llama-cpp-python, Qdrant, CUDA multi-GPU

Values

  • Clarity: Make complex systems understandable—for teammates, professors, and future collaborators.
  • Iteration: Learn fast through structured testing; every prototype has a hypothesis.
  • Measurable Impact: Connect every design decision to a verifiable outcome, from sensor deltas to material-waste percentages.
